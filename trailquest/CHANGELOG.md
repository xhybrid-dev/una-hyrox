# Changelog

All notable changes to Trail Quest are recorded here.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and
the project uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Releases are tagged `trailquest-vX.Y.Z`. `Software/cmake/trailquest-version.cmake` strips
the prefix for the SDK's version script.

### Added

- **The Ridge**: five stages from the valley to the summit, each with its own ground, weather,
  spirits and a boss, on a map with forks. The hut is a detour with a cache beside it.
- **Duels** with Surge, Settle, Skill and Fuel, a timing needle that moves each strike and
  changes with the move, the stage and the boss (it can be turned off on the title), and boss
  warnings before a charge or a guard.
- **A pack**: a beaten spirit may join (up to three); R1's Skill opens a pick list of the
  pack's moves with the damage and effect of each.
- **Fatigue carries over**: a stretch of trail gives back only a little, so Fuel (Gel, Water,
  Salt tab) or a hut is how you recover.
- **A story** at the key moments, in plain running terms, and help cards that teach the moves.
- **The summit's finale and a harder second lap.**
- **Jingles** on the buzzer's pitch (firmware 1.6 or newer).
- **Saving after every step**, with a backup copy; nothing leaves the watch.
